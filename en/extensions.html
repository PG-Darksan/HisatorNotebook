Mouse Gestures – Tab Control

Mouse Gestures – Tab Control

Hold the right mouse button, draw up, down, left, or right, and release to quickly switch, open, close, or reopen tabs, navigate back/forward, reload, or scroll.

  • 12 gesture shapes × 13 actions: Assign your favorite actions to 4 straight directions and 8 single-turn shapes, saved automatically on the spot
  • Live trail & action guide: See the cyan trail and target action name while drawing so you know the result before releasing (press Esc to cancel)
  • Zero external requests & lightweight: No browsing history collection or external network calls; settings are stored only inside your browser

Tempo – Video Playback Speed

Tempo – Video Playback Speed

Freely control HTML5 video playback speed on any web page from 0.25× up to 16× in 0.01× steps. Change all videos on a tab from the toolbar or control individual videos with the compact on-video panel.

  • 0.25× to 16× in 0.01× steps: One-tap 1×, 2×, 4×, 8×, and 16× presets plus fine adjustment via slider or number input
  • On-video mini panel & keyboard shortcuts: Unobtrusive collapsible overlay lets you control individual videos on the page
  • Speed memory & 12 languages: Remembers your chosen speed across tabs and sites, with a one-click OFF toggle to return to 1×
