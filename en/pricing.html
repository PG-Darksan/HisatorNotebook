Pricing

— Free / Pro / Max: find the plan that fits you

💳

Subscribe to paid plans in the app

To subscribe to a HisatorNotebook Pro / Max plan, launch the app and go to ⋮ (top right) → Plan & usage → Upgrade. Payments are processed securely by Stripe.
You can subscribe, change plans and cancel from within the app, but the checkout and subscription management screens open in your external browser.
Coupon codes for corporate billing or special campaigns can also be applied from the input field in the app.
Note: Please review the Notice under the Specified Commercial Transactions Act before purchasing.
